## Design Context

### Users

- **Library evaluators**: developers who land on the demo and decide whether `solid-slides` is worth adopting.
- **Conference attendees**: people watching a talk built with the library, often on a large screen from several meters away.
- **You (the creator)**: building a future "seed presentation" with the library, so the demo itself must be reusable and impressive.

The demo is both a product showcase and a real presentation tool.

### Brand Personality

**Precise yet bold yet understated.**

- **Precise**: clean hierarchy, deliberate spacing, every element earns its place.
- **Bold**: confident type, strong contrast, layouts that break the grid intentionally.
- **Understated**: no decoration for decoration's sake; warmth and tactility over flash.

The reference is **Anthropic Material**: editorial, warm, tactile, human. The anti-reference is generic AI-ism — the cold, cleanroom, OpenAI-style sameness.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual tone**: shadcn-inspired structure but with personality and warmth. Not a carbon-copy shadcn install.
- **Theme**: both light and dark modes. The demo should look considered in either context.
- **Typography**: `Valley Sans` for UI and body; `Clara` for display/editorial moments. Both fonts are installed locally and will be wired up via `@font-face` later.
- **Readability first**: this is a slides app, so all text must read from 10 ft away. Large type, strong contrast, and clear hierarchy are non-negotiable.
- **Color**: warm, tinted neutrals. Avoid cold grays and the cyan-purple neon AI palette. Use accent colors sparingly (60-30-10).
- **No AI slop**: no gradient text, no left-border accent stripes on cards, no identical icon-heading-text card grids, no glassmorphism without purpose, and no overuse of eyebrows.

### Design Principles

1. **Distance-readable first**: type, contrast, and spacing are tuned for a projector or large screen.
2. **Warm neutrality**: tint grays toward the brand hue; never use pure black or white.
3. **Editorial layout**: asymmetric compositions, varied spacing, and intentional grid breaks.
4. **Solid and intentional**: single solid colors for text; purposeful decorative elements only.
5. **Both modes, equally considered**: light and dark themes are first-class, not an afterthought.
6. **No generic component soup**: every pattern should feel made for this library, not pulled from a template.
